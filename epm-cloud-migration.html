Questions we hear

Before you book a call

How long does a typical Hyperion-to-Cloud migration take?
It depends on the number of modules and complexity of customizations. A focused HFM-to-FCCS migration can complete in 3 to 4 months. Multi-module migrations with complex integrations typically run 6 to 12 months.
Will my financial close be disrupted during migration?
No. We run parallel environments and schedule cutover outside critical reporting windows. Your team validates Cloud EPM against Hyperion before anything changes.
What happens to our Hyperion customizations?
We audit every customization and determine whether to replicate, redesign, or retire it in Cloud EPM. Nothing is lost without a documented decision.
Can you support us after migration?
Yes. We offer managed services for Cloud EPM so the team that knows your environment can operate it long-term.
Do we need to migrate everything at once?
No. We recommend a phased approach, starting with the module that delivers the most value or has the most urgency, then expanding from there.
