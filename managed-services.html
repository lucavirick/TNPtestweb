Questions we hear

Before you book a call

Which EPM platforms do you support?
Oracle Cloud EPM, Hyperion, OneStream, Anaplan, and more. We support planning, consolidation, reporting, close management, and data integration across platforms.
How does transition from our current vendor work?
We run a structured knowledge-transfer and shadowing period, and don't take ownership until runbooks and monitoring are in place. The timeline depends on complexity, but most transitions complete in 4 to 6 weeks.
Can you work alongside our internal team?
Yes. Most engagements are hybrid: we take operations and your team keeps strategy and business relationships, or the reverse.
What are your SLA tiers?
P1 through P4 with defined response and resolution targets. P1 incidents get a response in under 15 minutes. Full SLA details are covered in the assessment.
Where are your support teams located?
US-based account leadership and senior technical resources. Global coverage available for 24x7 operations.
Do you require a long contract?
No. Services can be scaled quarterly. We earn continued business through results, not contract lock-in.
